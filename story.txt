Professor Mehlhase was hundemuede from riding her drahtesel down Hauptstrasse.
The gebaeck and klimaanlage were just what she needed to recharge.
Her peace was short-lived, however, when an unruly Hund bolted into the
Biergarten and knocked an entire bowl of Sauerkraut all over her pristine Dirndel.
After a quick cleaning with the staubsauger, she decided she would rather ride
home in her Porsche.
Along the way, she decided to stop by a cafe to enjoy a warm slice of Apfelkuchen. 

Translated -
Professor Mehlhase was dog-tired from riding her iron horse down Main Street.
The backed goods and air conditioning were just what she needed to recharge.
Her peace was short-lived, however, when an unruly dog bolted into the
beer garden and knocked an entire bowl of Sauerkraut all over her pristine dirndl.
After a quick cleaning with the vacuum cleaner, she decided she would rather ride
home in her Porsche.
Along the way, she decided to stop by a cafe to enjoy a warm slice of apple cake.

Tip - A dirndl is a traditional German Dress.
